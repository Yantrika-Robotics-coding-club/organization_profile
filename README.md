> **Imagine. Build. Experiment. Repeat.**

Yantrika is a student-run robotics and coding club of the **Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS)**, under the Research Scholar Association.

Robotics brings together **physics, mathematics, electronics, computing, and engineering**. Yantrika aims to provide a space where students can move beyond learning individual concepts and use them to build complete systems.

We believe in learning through **experimentation, collaboration, and making things work**.

---

## Why Yantrika?

Robotics provides a unique way to turn theoretical concepts into working systems.

A concept from mechanics can become robot motion.
Linear algebra can become robot localization.
Probability can become sensor fusion.
Optics can become computer vision.
Algorithms can become autonomous navigation.

Yantrika aims to build a culture of:

* Hands-on experimentation
* Collaborative problem solving
* Open-ended projects
* Research-oriented engineering
* Learning through implementation

> **We don't just learn technology. We learn by building it.**

## Learn → Experiment → Build → Research

You do **not** need to be an expert to join Yantrika.

You don't need prior robotics experience, and you don't need to know every programming language.

Curiosity and the willingness to learn are enough to start.

Our learning process combines:

```text
Learn
  ↓
Experiment
  ↓
Build
  ↓
Research
```

Members can begin with fundamentals and gradually move toward complete robotic systems and independent projects.

---

## Workshops

Yantrika follows a structured, tutorial-based approach to learning.

Workshops are designed to be:

* Open to students with different levels of experience
* Step-by-step and fundamentals-oriented
* Focused on practical implementation
* Accompanied by hands-on activities

Learning does not end when a workshop ends.

Notes, materials, and simulations are intended to remain accessible through the club's GitHub repositories, allowing members to continue learning at their own pace.

---

## Building Real Systems

Yantrika combines software development with physical systems.

A typical development cycle can look like:

```text
Code → Simulate → Build → Test
```

Members can work with:

* Arduino and other microcontrollers
* C/C++ and embedded programming
* Sensors and actuators
* Electronics
* Robotics simulations
* Computer vision
* AI integration
* Autonomous systems

The goal is not simply to write code or assemble hardware independently, but to understand how the different components come together to form a functioning system.

# Projects
