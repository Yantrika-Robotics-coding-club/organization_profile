# Yantrika Projects

Yantrika's projects are designed to take members from learning individual concepts to building complete robotic systems.

Projects will be developed collaboratively, with members contributing across software, electronics, embedded systems, mechanics, sensing, and autonomous systems.

The focus is not only on the final prototype, but on the process of **learning, experimenting, building, testing, and improving**.

---

## How Projects Work

Each project will follow a general development cycle:

```text
Learn
  ↓
Experiment
  ↓
Design
  ↓
Simulate
  ↓
Build
  ↓
Test
  ↓
Improve
  ↓
Demonstrate
```

Projects may begin with small experiments or subsystems before being integrated into a complete working system.

A project can involve multiple teams working on different parts of the system simultaneously.

# Project 01 — Autonomous Drone

The first major Yantrika project is to build an **autonomous drone**.

The project will bring together multiple areas of robotics and engineering.

### Areas of Work

#### Programming & Algorithms

* C programming
* Control logic
* Algorithms

#### Embedded Electronics

* Microcontrollers
* Electronic interfaces
* ESCs
* Motors

#### Sensors

* IMU
* LiDAR
* GPS
* Cameras

#### Computer Vision

* Object detection
* Visual tracking

#### Autonomous Navigation

* Mapping
* Localization
* Path planning
* Autonomous navigation

The project will develop these components progressively before integrating them into a complete autonomous system.

---

# Project 02 — Autonomous Robot

The second major project is to build Yantrika's **first autonomous robot**.

The project will combine physical construction with sensing, computation, and autonomous decision-making.

### Areas of Work

#### Mechanical System

Design and construction of the robot's physical structure and movement system.

#### Embedded Electronics

Development of the electronics and embedded control required to operate the robot.

#### Sensors

Integration of sensors that allow the robot to perceive its surroundings.

#### Computer Vision

Using visual information as part of the robot's perception system.

#### Autonomous Navigation

Developing the capabilities required for the robot to navigate and operate autonomously.

# Future Projects

Yantrika is **not limited to these two projects**.

Members are encouraged to propose and develop their own ideas. Independent projects and new collaborative projects can become part of the club's project ecosystem.

Future projects may grow from:

* Ideas proposed by members
* Experiments conducted during workshops
* New technologies explored by the club
* Problems that members want to solve
* Extensions of existing projects

Projects will be showcased once they reach a **working prototype or a significant milestone**.

Each demonstration can include:

* The problem being addressed
* System design
* Implementation
* Working prototype
* Results
* Applications
* Future improvements

# Project Philosophy

Yantrika projects are not predefined products that members simply assemble.

They are systems that the club will **design, experiment with, build, and improve together**.

> **The robot hasn't been built yet. And that's the point.**

**Imagine. Build. Experiment. Repeat.**
